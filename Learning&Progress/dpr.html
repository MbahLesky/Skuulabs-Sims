Device Pixel Ratio (DPR)

The device pixel ratio (DPR) is a measure of how many physical pixels correspond to a single CSS pixel on a device. It is an important concept in web development, especially when working with high-resolution displays.

For example, a device with a DPR of 2 means that there are 2 physical pixels for every CSS pixel. This can affect how images and other visual elements are displayed on the screen.

Why does it matter?

Because if you don't account for the device pixel ratio, your images and graphics may appear blurry or pixelated on high-resolution displays. By using the correct DPR, you can ensure that your visuals look sharp and clear on all devices.

What does a high dpr mean?

A high device pixel ratio means that the device has a higher density of pixels, which can result in sharper and more detailed images. However, it also means that you need to provide higher resolution images to ensure they look good on these devices.
